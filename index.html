The Hidden Cost of the Wrong Material

Data Centers Are Scaling Fast. Your Materials Need to Keep Up.

Hyperscale expansion. AI-driven power loads. Increasingly complex cooling systems. The demands on data center infrastructure have never been higher — and the materials holding it all together are often an afterthought.

Steel corrodes. Fiberglass cracks. Off-the-shelf components don't fit. And when your cooling system fails or a containment unit leaks, the cost isn't just a repair order — it's downtime. There's a better way to build.

The Benefits of Plastic in Data Center Containment

Engineered for the Demands of Modern Data Centers

Polypropylene (PP) and High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) aren't new materials, but their application in data center infrastructure is one of the fastest-growing areas in custom fabrication. Here's why engineers, developers, and procurement teams are specifying them:

Zero Corrosion

No rust. No degradation. PP and HDPE hold up against chemicals, moisture, and time without the maintenance costs of metal.

Chemical Compatibility

From cooling treatment chemicals to DEF/Urea systems, these materials are engineered to handle aggressive substances without compromise.

Weldable & Fully Custom

Every component is fabricated to your exact specifications — tanks, trays, enclosures, panels, raceways — welded with precision to fit your facility, not molded from a generic catalog.

Lightweight Strength

High performance without the weight penalty of metal. Easier to install, easier to integrate, and easier to reconfigure as your facility evolves.

Factor Steel / Fiberglass Custom Poly (PP / HDPE)

What We Build for Data Centers

One Fabricator. Every Critical Application.

From the raised floor to the battery room, CPF fabricates custom PP and HDPE components across every phase of data center design and build-out. If it needs to be non-conductive, corrosion-proof, and built to your exact geometry — we make it.

Airflow Management & Containment

Where metal creates sharp edges and rigidity problems, custom plastic delivers — especially in legacy and non-standard layouts.

  • Hot aisle / cold aisle containment panels
  • Underfloor airflow baffles and return-air diverters
  • Custom blanking panels and aisle end caps
PP preferred — higher heat tolerance, stiffer under continuous load.

Drip Trays & Spill Containment

Welded seams. Leak-tested before delivery. No corrosion. No compliance risk.

  • CRAC / CRAH unit drip trays
  • Condensate collection pans
  • Chemical spill containment for chiller and pump areas
HDPE for impact-heavy zones. PP for chemical-intensive environments.

Cable Management & Electrical Isolation

Custom routing where standard metal trays don't fit — and where non-conductivity matters.

  • Underfloor cable troughs and overhead raceways
  • Power and fiber separation barriers
  • Custom cable waterfalls and drop boxes
PP preferred — non-conductive, low static buildup, clean installs.

Raised Floor & Access Components

Retrofit-heavy facilities reorder these constantly. We fabricate to the exact footprint every time.

  • Custom cut-out floor panels and removable access covers
  • Load-spreading plates and pedestal guards
  • Edge trims for saw-cut tiles and floor openings
HDPE for impact resistance. PP for load stability.

Battery Room & UPS Infrastructure

High compliance requirements. High chemical risk. No room for error — which is exactly why metal doesn't belong here.

  • Acid containment trays and battery rack liners
  • Neutralization sump boxes
  • Ventilation ducting for battery and UPS rooms
PP only — acid-resistant, fully welded seams, zero metal inserts.
